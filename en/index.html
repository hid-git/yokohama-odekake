Yokohama with Kids

A family guide for visitors to Yokohama, Japan — places little ones love, seasonal events, and the practical stuff: nursing rooms, diaper changing, strollers and kids' menus.

Before you go

  • Nursing rooms & diaper changing are everywhere in Japanese malls, big stations and museums — look for signs saying 授乳室 (nursing room) or ベビー休憩室 / 赤ちゃん休憩室 (baby room). Many have hot water for formula and a microwave.
  • Strollers: stations have elevators, but in Minato Mirai the walkways and malls are the easiest. Some malls lend strollers for free or for a small coin deposit.
  • Cash: cards and IC cards (Suica, PASMO) work widely, but small rides and some ticket machines are cash only — carry some coins.
  • Kids' menus: look for お子様メニュー or キッズメニュー. Family restaurants and food courts are the easiest with small kids.
  • Under-6s are often free: many zoos and museums here are free for preschoolers. Nogeyama Zoo is free for everyone.
  • Hours change: many places close one weekday a week and over New Year (around Dec 29 – Jan 3). Check the official link before you go.

Help & emergencies

  • Ambulance / fire: 119   Police: 110 (free from any phone)
  • Japan Visitor Hotline: 050-3816-2787 (from overseas +81-50-3816-2787) — 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, in English, Chinese and Korean. Help with accidents, illness, disasters and general tourist information. JNTO
  • Night clinic: Yokohama Night Emergency Center near Sakuragicho Station is open every night 20:00–24:00 (045-212-3535). Call before you go.
  • Find a hospital with English support: JNTO medical guide
